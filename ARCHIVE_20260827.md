The `v3.1.0-archive` release contains three split archives:

1. `RMDB_experiment_artifacts_v2_20260824-25.tar.gz.part-*`: V2 smoke tests, locked ablations, residual-initialised experiments, and RAV VascX-derived outputs.
2. `RMDB_experiment_artifacts_v3_20260825-26.tar.gz.part-*`: V3 residual experiments, native-resolution experiments, and the 12-epoch extension.
3. `RMDB_reverse_open_artifacts_20260821.tar.gz.part-*`: AFIO, Fundus-AVSeg, and RAV open-data reverse-validation outputs, including anatomy products, derived logits, occupancy probabilities, phenotypes, and audit reports.

All parts are verified by `RELEASE_20260827_SHA256.txt`.

## Data boundary

The earlier `v1.0.0` release remains the archive for redistributable source datasets and first-round checkpoints. Credentialed mBRSET content is not mirrored. Third-party pretrained weights are not relicensed or re-uploaded unless their redistribution terms are explicit. Virtual environments, package caches, and temporary render products are not scientific archive content and are excluded.

## Local cleanup rule

Local datasets, source-code worktrees, experiment outputs, historical manuscripts, and historical figure versions may be deleted only after the Git commit and all release assets are visible remotely and their hashes match the local manifest. The current manuscript `RMDB_3.1_Complete_FiguresIntegrated.docx` is retained locally.
