**Figure 2 | RMDB–BiRAP phenotype construction and outcome-blind reliability gating.** **A,** A real Fundus-AVSeg image (059_D) is standardised before measurement; optic-disc and foveal landmarks establish the retinal coordinate frame, and Zone B is constructed only when disc quality control passes. **B,** Two frozen branches retain the distinction between vascular identity and vessel support. VascX with the two-step UDCT corrector estimates artery, vein and crossing identity, whereas the FIVES-derived occupancy estimator quantifies vessel support without assigning identity. Mass-UDCT retains calibrated four-class probability mass, whereas Factorized-UDCT requires occupancy support before identity is expressed. Prespecified operators generate candidate phenotypes while preserving entropy and disagreement between readouts. **C,** BiRAP applies ICC(1,1) to same-eye repeated measurements before clinical labels are modelled. Four of nine prespecified phenotypes met the threshold of ICC(1,1) ≥ 0.60 and were aggregated as bilateral patient-level summaries using patient-grouped folds; features failing quality control or repeatability stop before outcome modelling.
