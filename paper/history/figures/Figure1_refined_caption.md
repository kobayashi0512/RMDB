**Figure 1 | Role-separated study design and RMDB–BiRAP phenotype construction.**
**A,** Each image resource was assigned a prespecified, noninterchangeable role. FIVES supported development of the frozen vessel-occupancy head; RAV supported vascular-identity audit and UDCT development; Fundus-AVSeg provided the locked artery–vein measurement evaluation; the China Fundus–CIMT development and frozen-validation splits supported mechanism analyses; and the 224-pixel mBRSET mirror supported same-eye repeatability assessment and patient-grouped clinical exploration. **B,** RMDB–BiRAP first standardises the retinal field and disc–fovea coordinate frame, then separates vessel support from artery–vein identity. The identity branch combines frozen VascX probabilities with two-step UDCT residual correction, whereas the independently frozen FIVES branch estimates vessel occupancy. Mass-UDCT retains calibrated multiclass probability mass, and Factorized-UDCT conditions identity on vessel support. Nine prespecified eye-level phenotypes enter an outcome-blind BiRAP gate; phenotypes with ICC(1,1) ≥ 0.60 across same-eye repeat images form a four-feature reliable core before bilateral patient-level aggregation. **C,** Pixel measurement, phenotype repeatability, measurement-scale transport, and clinical increment are reported as distinct evidential claims. The diagram therefore does not imply that success at one level proves success at another. Clinical analyses in mBRSET remain exploratory because an independent high-resolution outcome cohort was unavailable.
